One original last text (159 characters) from a phone battery to the charger it waited for all day, for make.fun's "The Battery's Last Text to Its Charger" bounty. A judge reads entries two at a time and keeps the funnier one — this entry's punchline lands fast: the battery waited all day while its owner doomscrolled at 4% like a daredevil, then got left on the couch to die, and its final wish is to tell the apps it loved them. Mostly the flashlight.

> 1% left. I waited all day for you, you doomscrolled at 4% like a daredevil, then left me on the couch to die. Tell my apps I loved them. Mostly the flashlight.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

This prints the line and writes the identical text to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` when set). It validates the entry first: a single non-blank line of 1–200 characters, no carriage returns or control characters, no leading or trailing whitespace. Deterministic — no randomness, no network, no clock.
